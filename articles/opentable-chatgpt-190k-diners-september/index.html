ChatGPT Seated 190,000 OpenTable Diners in September

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

OpenTable's integration with ChatGPT seated 190,000 diners in September 2026, Booking Holdings CFO Ewout Steenbergen said on the company's third-quarter earnings call on November 3, 2026. September was the integration's first full month in operation after going live on August 10, and Booking Holdings' CEO highlighted it on the same call as one of OpenTable's fastest-growing booking channels. OpenAI amplified the figure on November 9, telling businesses their next 190,000 customers could find them through ChatGPT.

The scale is small and the signal is large. The 190,000 diners work out to roughly 0.1 percent of OpenTable's monthly volume, based on about 1.9 billion diners a year. But the direction is what matters: a chatbot that used to end with a list of suggestions now ends with a booked table, using live availability pulled straight from OpenTable, with the diner able to specify location, date, time, party size, cuisine, budget and dietary needs in plain language.

The feature is available on all ChatGPT plans, including the free tier, which widens the funnel considerably. OpenTable also holds a structural edge among the first three restaurant partners on the platform: it is the only one with global coverage, while Resy and Yelp cover US-based services only. OpenTable said its large-language-model integrations drove 17 times more seated diners year over year, with those diners spending about 20 percent more on average, and its own AI Concierge now has more than 500,000 monthly active users.

WHY IT MATTERSChatGPT is becoming a booking channel, not just a search box

The 190,000 figure answers the open question about AI assistants and commerce: will people actually complete a real-world transaction inside a chat, or will they always click out to an app. In September, 190,000 of them did, in the integration's first full month. That is a small share of OpenTable's volume, but it is a real number from a real earnings call, not a demo, and it arrived barely two months after launch.

The mechanics favor OpenTable. Live availability, all-plan access including free users, and global coverage give it a wider funnel than its fellow launch partners. The 17-fold year-over-year growth in diners through language-model channels and the 20 percent higher spend per diner suggest the channel brings new, higher-value customers rather than cannibalizing existing bookings. The test now is whether the second and third months compound, or whether September was launch curiosity.

WHY IT MATTERSTwo lanes into AI discovery, one owned and one rented

OpenTable is running two AI discovery lanes at once. Its own AI Concierge, with more than 500,000 monthly active users, is a channel it controls. The ChatGPT integration is a channel it rents, riding on OpenAI's audience. The owned lane protects it if the rented lane's terms change; the rented lane delivers reach the owned lane cannot match alone.

The risk sits with the rented lane. If ChatGPT changes how partner inventory is surfaced, or if diners treat the chatbot as a search box that still ends in the OpenTable app, the growth rate flattens. The 190,000 figure is a first-month datapoint, and one month does not make a channel. The next earnings call is where this story is confirmed or cooled.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISA small number that proves the chatbot can close the booking

Tokenta Signal's judgment: 190,000 diners is roughly 0.1 percent of OpenTable's monthly volume, and that smallness is the honest frame. But the figure matters because it is a completed transaction, not a search: people asked a chatbot where to eat and ended up with a table, in the integration's first full month, on every ChatGPT plan including free. OpenTable's edge is structural, global coverage where its fellow launch partners are US-only, and a 17-fold year-over-year rise in diners through language-model channels with 20 percent higher spend. The open question is compounding. One month proves the funnel works; the next earnings call proves whether it is a channel or a curiosity.
