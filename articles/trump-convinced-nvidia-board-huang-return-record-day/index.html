WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

President Donald Trump said on November 23, 2026 that he convinced Nvidia's board to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive, telling CNBC he spoke with the board directly and indirectly and gave it the national-security assurance it had asked for. The Wall Street Journal reported that Trump asked Vice President JD Vance to work over the weekend to persuade the board, and that Vance, after consulting Attorney General Todd Blanche, told directors the government did not consider Huang a national security risk.

Nvidia shares closed at $165.05, up $39.52, or 31.48%, on November 23, MarketWatch reported — the stock's best day since May 9, 2003 and the third-best in its history. The gain added $952 billion in market value, the largest one-day gain for a US company. The Nasdaq Composite rose 6.5%, its best day since April 2025, adding more than $2 trillion in market value.

The probes continue. California Attorney General Rob Bonta's office said on November 23 that Huang's reinstatement does not diminish the need for its investigation into what Nvidia told investors about its China ties, Reuters reported. The Securities and Exchange Commission is examining Nvidia's accounting disclosures, and the Justice Department's criminal investigation into Megaspeed continues. A petition calling for Huang's reinstatement passed 200,000 signatures, The Times reported.

WHY IT MATTERSA presidency inside a boardroom.

A sitting president says he convinced a public company's board to rehire its chief executive, and the vice president delivered the national-security assurance the board said it needed before voting unanimously. The board had said a week earlier that it did not intend to rehire Huang. The episode raises direct questions about board independence at one of the world's most valuable companies.

The reaction in Congress was swift. Republican congressman Don Bacon said on X that Huang was compromised and that Congress needs to establish who approved high-end chip sales to China; Democratic congresswoman Yassamin Ansari said Huang and the board needed to come before Congress and the public immediately. Huang is due to be questioned by three House committees on December 1.

THE MARKET VERDICTRelief, priced in a single session.

The 31.48% gain is the third-best day in Nvidia's history and the largest one-day market-value gain ever for a US company, at $952 billion. The rally snapped a seven-session losing streak and reversed part of a slide of about a third in two weeks. Short sellers lost about $11.2 billion on paper, the worst one-day loss for Nvidia shorts in data going back to 2019, MarketWatch reported.

The rally still leaves Nvidia shares well below their early-October record high, MarketWatch reported. The market has settled the leadership question for now. It has not settled the legal one: the California probe, the SEC review and the Megaspeed investigation all remain open.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISThe rally settles the leadership question, not the legal one.

The market treated the reinstatement as the removal of the single biggest uncertainty in the AI trade, and priced it in a single session: the best day in 23 years and the largest one-day market-value gain in US history. The political price is separate. A board that reversed itself after a presidential intervention, and a chief executive who returns with criminal and civil probes still open, leave governance questions the rally does not answer. The leadership question is settled. The legal one is not.
