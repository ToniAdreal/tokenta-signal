Claude Code Product Lead Cat Wu Joins OpenAI to Run Core Products, Third Exit in Ten Days

Catherine "Cat" Wu, who led product for Claude Code and Cowork at Anthropic until October 30, 2026, announced she is joining OpenAI to lead product management for Core Products, working with Codex lead Thibault Sottiaux on team agents. She is the third Claude Code departure in ten days, arriving just as Anthropic's IPO is planned for this month.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Catherine "Cat" Wu, who led product for Claude Code and Cowork at Anthropic, announced on November 4, 2026 that she is joining OpenAI to lead product management for Core Products. Her last day at Anthropic was October 30, 2026, one day before she announced her exit and one day ahead of the OpenAI announcement.

At OpenAI, Wu said she will work with Codex lead Thibault Sottiaux. She framed the brief in her announcement as building agents that have your back and your team's, and keep you in control, so people get more time for the people and work they love.

The move follows two other Claude Code departures within ten days. Thariq Shihipar and Anthony Morris, who built Claude Code on desktop, both announced their exits on October 24, 2026, followed by Wu. None of the three gave a reason for leaving, as reported by both coverage sources.

The timing draws attention because Anthropic's IPO is planned for this month. Wu addressed the timing question directly, saying she had no idea whether leaving before the IPO changes any lockup treatment and that it was not a factor in her decision.

WHY IT MATTERSOpenAI is hiring the product brain behind its coding rival

Claude Code grew from a small internal Anthropic tool into a coding and agent product that competes directly with OpenAI's Codex. Wu steered that product from its internal-tool days, when most users approved every permission prompt manually, to a default of trusting Claude to auto-approve. OpenAI is not just adding a product manager, it is importing the person who made that product judgment at its chief rival.

Her stated brief, team agents that keep users in control, sits exactly on the fault line between the two companies' coding products. Codex and Claude Code are converging on agentic coding, and the differentiator is increasingly how much autonomy users hand to the agent. Wu's move gives OpenAI first-hand knowledge of how Anthropic answered that question.

WHY IT MATTERSThree exits in ten days is a pattern, not a coincidence

A single departure is a career move. Three departures from the same product team in ten days, with no reasons given, reads as a signal about the team itself, whether it is compensation ahead of an IPO, disagreements over product direction, or simply the pull of rival offers. Anthropic confirmed Wu's departure but has not explained the cluster.

The watchpoint is whether the exits stop at three or continue into the IPO window. If more Claude Code leaders follow, Anthropic faces a retention story at the worst possible moment, just as its flagship coding product becomes a central pillar of its public-market pitch.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISA talent transfer that doubles as a competitive tell

Tokenta Signal's judgment: the headline is not one hire, it is what the hire reveals. OpenAI bought the product judgment behind Claude Code's rise, and Anthropic lost a third leader from the same team in ten days without an explanation. The watchpoint is whether the exits stop at three or become an IPO-window retention story.
