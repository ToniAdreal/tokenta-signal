Microchip Q2 Sales Jump 42% to $1.62B, Topping Guidance

Microchip reported fiscal Q2 2027 net sales of $1.622 billion, up 42.2% year over year and above guidance, with non-GAAP EPS of $0.97 beating consensus; the December quarter was guided flat to down.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Microchip Technology reported second-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales of $1.622 billion on November 5, 2026, up 42.2% year over year from $1.14 billion and up 9.2% sequentially, above the high end of its own guidance range of $1.589 billion to $1.618 billion. GAAP net income was $373.6 million, or $0.68 per diluted share, against guidance of $0.53 to $0.54, with GAAP gross profit of 65.9% and operating income of $464.5 million. Non-GAAP earnings were $0.97 per diluted share, above the $0.91 to $0.95 guidance range and the $0.94 consensus, on non-GAAP operating income of $639.9 million.

The December quarter looks softer. Microchip guided net sales to a range of down 3% to up 1% sequentially, a midpoint of down 1%, and flagged that about $53 million of infrequent revenue in the September quarter will not repeat. The board declared a quarterly cash dividend of 45.5 cents per share, payable December 8, 2026 to holders of record on November 23, 2026. Design wins for PCIe Gen6 switches and retimers rose to 24 from 12 in the June quarter. Shares fell 13.9% in the regular session on November 5, before the results crossed after the close, in a broad selloff across semiconductor names.

WHY IT MATTERSA 42% recovery with a flat guide attached

The 42.2% year-over-year sales jump is the strongest print of Microchip's recovery from the distribution inventory correction, and a non-GAAP operating margin of 39.5% shows operating expenses growing well below sales. Beating the high end of guidance while topping the earnings consensus by three cents is a clean quarter on its own terms.

The guide is the other half. A December quarter guided to down 3% to up 1% sequentially, with $53 million of one-time revenue dropping out, reads as management refusing to extrapolate September's strength. The 13.9% session drop came before the release, so it was a verdict on the sector-wide selloff rather than on these numbers.

WHY IT MATTERSPCIe Gen6 design wins are the AI data-center tell

Design wins for PCIe Gen6 switches and retimers doubled to 24 from 12 in a single quarter, the clearest product signal in the release. Microchip's data-center portfolio, built for AI infrastructure demand, is where the recovery is supposed to compound, and design wins are the leading indicator that it is.

The risk is the one analysts flagged ahead of the print: foundry capacity constraints, longer lead times and rising manufacturing costs. A 42% growth quarter built on a recovering cycle can stall quickly if supply cannot keep up with the order book.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISA strong recovery quarter, priced against a flat winter

Tokenta Signal's judgment: $1.622 billion, up 42%, with a 39.5% non-GAAP operating margin, is a genuine recovery print, and the PCIe Gen6 design-win doubling gives the AI data-center story something concrete. But a December guide of flat to down, with $53 million of one-time revenue falling away, means the recovery is not yet a straight line. The quarter earns the beat; the guide earns the caution.
