Nvidia Board Rules Out Huang's Return, Hires Heidrick & Struggles to Find Its Next CEO

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Nvidia's board used its first earnings call without Jensen Huang to rule out his return as chief executive, hours after Huang said he would be honored to come back if asked. Chairman Harvey Jones opened the November 17, 2026 call by telling analysts that the board unanimously accepted Huang's resignation on November 12 and "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO". Sources had told CNBC on Monday that the board was not considering a reinstatement; the call made that position official.

The statement landed against a public campaign for Huang's return. Huang said earlier on Tuesday that he would be honored to return if the board decided it was in the best interest of shareholders, employees, the company and America, and investors including Elon Musk and Bill Ackman have gathered signatures for his reinstatement, while President Donald Trump said he would not mind Huang returning. The board answered all of it with one sentence, and added that it has retained executive search firm Heidrick & Struggles to lead the search for a permanent chief executive, considering internal and external candidates.

Jones also closed off the subject for the evening. "We will not be addressing or taking questions on today's call regarding the circumstances of Jensen's resignation and the ongoing investigation," he said before handing the call to interim chief executive Colette Kress. The independent board investigation continues, the Wall Street Journal has reported that the Securities and Exchange Commission is examining Nvidia, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has said he wants answers from the board within 10 days. Earlier on Tuesday the board created a committee to oversee national security and export control compliance.

The business case Kress presented was undiminished. "Our momentum is real, with the market-leading multi-year roadmap, supply agreements extending through to the end of the decade and sustainable demand, we are well positioned to accelerate Nvidia's leadership position," she said. Agentic AI is driving extraordinary demand for CPUs, Vera Rubin is targeted at satisfying as much of that demand as possible, and AI enterprise demand is expected to double in fiscal 2028. Kress also said Nvidia is no longer shipping its Hopper generation of AI processors to China. Shares swung sharply after hours, falling at first before recovering to trade nearly 3 percent higher during the call.

WHY IT MATTERSThe comeback trade is closed

The board's statement removes the one scenario that had given Nvidia's stock a second story. Huang's offer to return, the investor campaign behind it and the president's blessing had kept a comeback priced in as a possibility; the board has now said on the record that it will not happen. That leaves the company with an interim chief executive, an open search, an investigation it will not discuss and regulators asking questions, all while the demand numbers keep rising.

The governance discount is now the question the market has to price. The quarter itself was a beat, with $115.8 billion in revenue and a $130 billion fourth-quarter guide, and the stock recovered in after-hours trading, but the early dip after Jones spoke showed investors care about leadership as much as demand. Until a permanent chief executive is named, the AI demand story has to carry the stock without the founder who built the franchise.

WHAT TO WATCHThe search clock and the investigation

Watch the search first. Jones said the board wants to install a permanent chief executive as quickly as possible but will not expedite the choice before finding exactly the right person, and no timeline was given. Whether the pick comes from inside Nvidia or outside it will say how much of Huang's strategy the board intends to keep, and how fast the governance discount fades.

Watch the two clocks running alongside it. Bessent's 10-day window for answers from the board runs to about November 25, and Huang is due to face lawmakers on December 1 in the House and December 3 in the Senate Banking Committee. Kress's promise that AI enterprise demand doubles in fiscal 2028 is the case for the stock in the meantime, and the next guide is where that promise gets tested.
