Apple Releases a Video Model; One Frame Takes Just 36 Milliseconds

Apple is not competing on the strongest video model but on the fastest. Once video generation is fast enough to respond in real time, the game shifts from producing clips to producing worlds.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

On November 16, 2026, Apple's machine learning research group put the FastFrames weights on Hugging Face. The code and paper followed the next day, arXiv number 2611.23226. Two models were released at once: the smaller FastFrames-S at 1.3 billion parameters and the larger FastFrames-L at 14 billion parameters.

Both are two-step autoregressive video diffusion models, outputting 480p video at 16 frames per second, 81 frames at a time. Apple's reported speed: the S version produces a frame in about 36 milliseconds on a single B200, and the L version in about 45 milliseconds on eight B200s. The L version was distilled from Wan2.1-14B and scores 84.44 overall on VBench.

At this speed, the way video generation is used changes. Instead of rendering a whole clip in one pass and handing it over, the model emits frames one by one, taking keyboard and drag inputs and responding in real time. The same release includes a LingBot 1.3B, 16 times faster than the official version, as an interactive demo for camera control.

WHAT TO WATCHFast, but with a threshold

Running it is not easy. The official install notes require Linux x86-64 with an NVIDIA GPU and CUDA 12.8 drivers. Macs cannot run it, and the headline speed numbers come from B200-class hardware.

The L version has 14.3B parameters, and the DiT weights alone are 28.6GB. The repository totals 174GB, of which 143GB are intermediate checkpoints on the distillation chain. Check the disk before downloading.

WHY IT MATTERSApple is not competing on strongest, but on fastest

FastFrames is under the Apple Machine Learning Research Model License, research only, not commercial use. Apple is not selling a product this time; what it released is a research signal.

The signal is clear: once a video model is fast enough to respond in real time, the game shifts from producing clips to producing worlds. The interactive world-model line now has another reference point, this one from Apple.
