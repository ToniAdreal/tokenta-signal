Qualys Beats on Q3 Revenue and Lifts Its Outlook as AI Security Tools Gain Ground

Qualys reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $188.0 million, up 11 percent year over year and ahead of the $186.6 million consensus, with GAAP net income of $57.2 million and non-GAAP earnings per share of $2.12, as it launched AI-led security products including InstaScan and expanded TotalAI governance. The company raised full-year revenue guidance to $736.9 million to $738.9 million and non-GAAP earnings per share guidance to $8.07 to $8.14.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Qualys reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $188.0 million on November 9, 2026, up 11 percent from $169.9 million a year earlier and ahead of the $186.6 million consensus. GAAP net income rose 14 percent to $57.2 million, or $1.62 per diluted share, while non-GAAP net income rose 11 percent to $74.8 million, or $2.12 per diluted share. Adjusted EBITDA climbed 9 percent to $89.9 million, a 48 percent margin, and operating cash flow rose 13 percent to $101.8 million.

The quarter's product news is where Qualys is placing its bet. The company introduced InstaScan, powered by Agent Insta, delivering scanless vulnerability detection within minutes of an advisory disclosure, and advanced TruRisk Eliminate with AI-powered Patch Reliability Scoring, wave-based autonomous remediation, and peer-to-peer patching. It also expanded TotalAI with governance capabilities that help security chiefs reduce shadow AI, flag abnormal model behavior, and discover AI agents and MCP servers, with red-team testing against OWASP Top 10 and EU AI Act standards. Chief executive Sumedh Thakar pointed to rising adoption of the AI-native Risk Operations Center as the driver.

Qualys raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $736.9 million to $738.9 million, up from $732.0 million to $738.0 million, and lifted full-year non-GAAP earnings per share guidance to $8.07 to $8.14, up from $7.74 to $7.88. Fourth-quarter revenue is guided at $191.0 million to $193.0 million, growth of 9 to 10 percent. Shares rose 6.3 percent to $202.46 in Monday's regular session, ahead of the release after the close.

WHY IT MATTERSThe AI pitch is now the growth story

Every product highlight in this release carries an AI label: an AI-native Risk Operations Center, AI-powered patch scoring, autonomous remediation, and governance for AI agents and MCP servers. That is not decoration. Qualys is repositioning a vulnerability-management business as the control layer for the frontier AI era, and the quarter's analyst recognition, the highest score for current offering in Forrester's 2026 Wave for Proactive Security Platforms and Leader status in IDC's MarketScape for DAST, gives the pitch third-party backing.

The test is whether the AI framing converts into faster growth. Revenue up 11 percent is solid and profitable, with a 30 percent GAAP net margin, but it is the same pace Qualys has run for quarters. The AI products are pitched as the reason the next leg is faster, and the raised guidance is a small step in that direction, not proof of it.

WHY IT MATTERSA steady beat, not a breakout

Read honestly, this is a narrow beat: revenue topped the $186.6 million consensus by less than 1 percent, and the fourth-quarter guide of 9 to 10 percent growth implies a slight deceleration from the 11 percent just printed. The guidance raise is real but modest, about $5 million at the midpoint on a $737 million year, and the earnings-per-share raise is the larger move.

The watchpoint is the gap between the AI narrative and the growth rate. If InstaScan and TotalAI start pulling new customers and expanding existing contracts, billings and revenue growth should bend upward over the next two quarters. If growth stays near 10 percent, Qualys remains a well-run, highly profitable security vendor whose AI story is marketing ahead of momentum. For now, the numbers support the first reading and leave the second open.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISA solid, profitable quarter, with the AI story still ahead of the growth rate

Tokenta Signal's judgment: The headline is a clean, profitable beat with a raised outlook, and the 30 percent GAAP net margin is the number that separates Qualys from growth-at-any-cost peers. But the beat itself was narrow, under 1 percent on revenue, and the fourth-quarter guide implies growth easing to 9 to 10 percent. The AI-native Risk Operations Center, InstaScan, and TotalAI are a coherent pitch for where security spending is heading, and the analyst recognition backs it, yet none of it has bent the growth rate upward so far. Qualys is a durable, cash-generative vendor whose AI story is credible but unproven in the numbers. The next two quarters decide whether the pitch becomes momentum.
