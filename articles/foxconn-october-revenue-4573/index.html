Foxconn October Revenue Jumps 45.7% as AI Server Demand Holds

Foxconn's October 2026 revenue rose 45.73% year over year, Reuters reported on November 5, implying about NT$1.31 trillion on the prior-year base of NT$895.7 billion, a second straight month above NT$1 trillion after September's record NT$1.16 trillion.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Hon Hai Precision Industry, known as Foxconn, reported October 2026 revenue up 45.73% year over year, according to a Reuters item published November 5, 2026. The wire headline carries the growth rate; the absolute figure had not yet appeared in a second published report when this flash was written.

Applied to Hon Hai's own reported October 2025 base of NT$895.7 billion, the 45.73% rise implies October 2026 revenue of about NT$1.31 trillion. That would be the second straight month above NT$1 trillion, after September's NT$1.1586 trillion, up 38.42% year over year, and it would top September's record monthly figure.

The print extends the AI server story that drove the third quarter, when revenue reached NT$3.0269 trillion, up 47.12% year over year and above the NT$2.83 trillion LSEG SmartEstimate. Hon Hai has said AI-related operations are expected to keep growing in the fourth quarter alongside the traditional second-half peak season. Full third-quarter earnings, with margins and mix, are due November 12.

WHY IT MATTERSA second trillion-dollar month would confirm the demand is structural, not a one-off

September's first NT$1 trillion month could have been a spike. A second month above that line, growing faster year over year at 45.73% than September's 38.42%, reads as sustained pull-in for AI racks rather than a single quarter's catch-up. Foxconn is the largest assembler of Nvidia's AI systems, so its monthly revenue is one of the fastest public reads on data-center hardware demand.

The comparison base matters too. October 2025 was NT$895.7 billion, a strong month in its own right. Growing 45.73% against that base means the absolute increment is roughly NT$410 billion in a single month, larger than most contract manufacturers' entire monthly revenue.

WHY IT MATTERSRevenue is the appetizer; November 12 is the test

Monthly sales say nothing about margins. The third quarter showed the tension: revenue up 47% while the market still waited for proof that operating leverage follows. The November 12 earnings report will show whether the AI rack volume is converting into profit or only into top-line scale.

Until then, the October figure's job is narrower: it tells investors the order book did not cool after the record quarter. With no numerical forecast from the company, each monthly print is the only forward signal until earnings.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISThe AI rack line is still accelerating into the fourth quarter

Tokenta Signal's judgment: a 45.73% year-over-year rise in October, faster than September's 38.42%, means the AI server surge did not pause after the record quarter. The derived NT$1.31 trillion would be a second trillion-dollar month in a row, and the increment alone is larger than most rivals' monthly sales. The number to watch next is not another revenue print but the November 12 earnings, where margins will show whether this volume is profitable growth or just scale.
