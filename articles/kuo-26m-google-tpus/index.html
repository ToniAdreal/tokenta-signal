26 Million TPUs, an Estimate No One Has Claimed

The numbers come from a supply-chain survey, not a company disclosure. The dollar commitments in Anthropic's prospectus point in the same direction; the chip count still has no company behind it.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

On November 16, Ming-Chi Kuo posted on X that his latest industry survey shows new orders from OpenAI and Anthropic extending Google TPU shipment visibility to 2029, ahead of Nvidia GPUs and other AI custom chips. The post went up at 01:02 UTC. Kuo wrote: "Strong new orders from OpenAI and Anthropic have extended Google TPU shipment visibility to 2029, ahead of Nvidia GPUs and other AI ASICs (mostly 4Q27–2Q28)."

The split is the core of the post. OpenAI takes about 12 million units, TPU 8t, designed by MediaTek, bought outright. Anthropic takes about 14 million, TPU 8i, designed by Broadcom, a mix of leasing and outright purchase. Both labs' demand is growing year by year.

Kuo also gave shipment estimates: 8 million to 9 million units in 2027, 14 million to 15 million in 2028, and 16 million to 18 million in 2029. The two labs' combined ~26 million in 2028–2029 would be 79% to 87% of his estimated shipments — a figure obtained by dividing one set of estimates by the other, not a share disclosed by Google.

WHAT TO WATCHAnthropic's Prospectus Gives Dollars, Not Chip Counts

Anthropic's amended prospectus, filed November 10, commits to at least $121.3 billion under its Google hosting agreement from April 2026 to July 2033, plus $125 billion to $150 billion of TPU compute expected to be delivered in 2028–2031. Broadcom equipment lease rents run to about $161.2 billion.

The filing contains no chip count for 2028 or 2029. The dollar commitments and Kuo's unit estimates point in the same direction, and neither can serve as evidence for the other.

WHY IT MATTERSGoogle's TPU Is Turning From an In-House Chip Into a Product

From 2028, Google's own compute expansion moves to the next-generation v9, and the v8 orders are, in Kuo's reading, largely from OpenAI and Anthropic. MediaTek holds the exclusive design for v9 and v10, accounting for 45% to 60% of total TPU supply in 2027–2029.

Nvidia's competition is changing. For two years Google's TPU served only Google; now it has large external customers, and Nvidia faces a rival that is no longer just another chip company.

THE SHIFTWhat Changed

FROM IN-HOUSE CHIP TO PRODUCT FOR SALE
From an in-house chip to a product for sale
Google's TPU used to serve only Google. Kuo's post sketches a chip supplier whose two biggest customers are outside labs.
FROM ONE SUPPLIER TO CUSTOM SILICON
From Nvidia dominance to custom silicon taking share
If the two labs really take 26 million TPUs, the frontier compute supply picture is no longer Nvidia's alone. Custom silicon's share has a concrete order of magnitude for the first time.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISA Weighty Estimate, but Not Yet a Confirmed Order

Tokenta Signal's judgment: Kuo's 26 million is a weighty estimate, not a confirmed order. The hundreds of billions of dollars of compute commitments in Anthropic's prospectus show the direction of demand is real; the precision of the count is another matter. Google turning the TPU from an in-house chip into a product for sale has already happened — the 26 million figure only counts once a company says it out loud.

WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAINWhat Remains Uncertain

The 26 million, 12 million and 14 million figures are all Ming-Chi Kuo's estimates based on industry surveys; Google, OpenAI and Anthropic have confirmed none of them. The 79% to 87% range is the arithmetic result of dividing one set of estimates by the other. This does not constitute investment advice.
