Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, 11 Days After His Resignation

The board said it was satisfied with Huang's candor and ability to lead and, after input from senior levels of the US government, is confident the return poses no national security risk. WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

NVIDIA announced on November 23, 2026 that its board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and chief executive officer and as a member of the board, effective immediately, 11 days after he resigned. The board said it was satisfied with respect to Huang's candor and ability to lead, and that after receiving input from senior levels of the United States government it is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk.

The reversal follows mounting pressure from employees, shareholders and business partners, Reuters reported, as well as from President Donald Trump, who said last week he wanted Huang back in the top job. A petition calling for reinstatement drew more than 180,000 signatures, including one from Elon Musk. Board chair Harvey Jones had said last week the company would not rehire Huang.

Shares surged more than 31% in premarket trading on November 23, Reuters reported, after losing about a third of their value in the prior two weeks. Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, continues as chief financial officer, and Harvey Jones remains board chair.

WHY IT MATTERSThe co-founder is the strategy.

Huang built Nvidia from a video-game chip designer into the dominant supplier of artificial intelligence processors, playing a central role in its chip roadmap, major customer relationships and ties with Washington. The board's decision to bring him back highlights how dependent one of the world's most valuable companies remains on its co-founder.

The episode raises questions about governance. The board accepted Huang's resignation on November 12, said days later it did not intend to reinstate him, and then voted unanimously to bring him back within two weeks, under pressure from shareholders, employees and the White House.

THE CLEARANCEWashington signed off, the investigation continues.

The board said it received input from senior levels of the United States government and, based on that input, is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk. President Trump welcomed the decision on Truth Social, saying the board made the correct and proper decision.

The clearance is not a closure. The independent-director committee investigating reporting that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort will proceed to conclusion, and Huang has committed to cooperating. NVIDIA also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee and is searching for a new Chief Compliance Officer.

WHAT IS NEXTA return, not a resolution.

Watch for the findings of the independent committee's investigation, for export-control scrutiny of Nvidia's China business, and for how Huang handles the congressional appearances already scheduled, including a House interview on December 1 and a Senate Banking hearing on December 3.

The market verdict was immediate: the premarket surge shows investors see Huang as inseparable from Nvidia's AI roadmap. The governance test is whether the new compliance structures hold up under the same scrutiny that forced his resignation.
