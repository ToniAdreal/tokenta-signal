Tencent Releases Open Weights for Hy4, Its 770B-Parameter Flagship, Under a New Licence

Tencent released the open weights for Hy4 on November 20, 2026, fulfilling the promise made at the model's November 4 launch. The natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model carries 770 billion total parameters with 49 billion active per token and a 1-million-token context window. Three checkpoints are live on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode, alongside a system card with a safety evaluation report. The weights ship under a new Tencent Hy License, not Apache 2.0, with extra commercial terms for large model-as-a-service businesses.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Tencent released the open weights for Hy4 on November 20, 2026, closing the loop on the flagship model it launched on November 4 as an API-only model. The announcement went out from the Tencent Hy account on X: "Today, we're releasing the open weights for Hy4. Our natively multimodal flagship MoE (770B total / 49B active, 1M context). Alongside the Hy4 System Card with a comprehensive safety evaluation report." Three checkpoints are live on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode: the instruction-tuned Hy4, an FP8-quantised Hy4-FP8, and the pre-trained Hy4 base. The shard files total about 1.55TB for Hy4, 0.79TB for the FP8 copy and 1.54TB for the base model.

Hy4 is a mixture-of-experts model, so most of it sits idle for any one token. The language backbone has 78 layers: one dense layer, then 77 layers that each hold 256 routed experts and one shared expert. A router picks 8 routed experts per token, which works out to 49 billion active parameters, plus 1.23 billion for the vision encoder that lets the model read images and video. For long inputs, Hy4 uses Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention with IndexCache, and it ships with DFly2, a 3.3-billion-parameter drafter for speculative decoding. Both vLLM and SGLang have prebuilt images, and the repository carries fine-tuning scripts for DeepSpeed, LLaMA-Factory and ms-swift.

Tencent's own numbers put the base model ahead of its predecessor and roughly level with DeepSeek's flagship base model. Scored few-shot by Tencent, Hy4 Base records 91.6 on MMLU against 87.5 for Hy3 preview Base and 91.1 for DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base, with 73.9 on MMLU-Pro, 62.7 on SuperGPQA, 57.2 on SimpleQA and 71.6 on U-MATH. It trails on coding: 74.0 on MBPP-plus against 79.1 for DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base. For the instruct model, Tencent cites a blind test of 579 financial tasks judged by 213 finance professionals, where Hy4 scored 3.04 out of 4 against 2.97 for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 and 2.68 each for Kimi K3.1 Pro and GLM-5.3. Tencent says Hy4 cut its hallucination rate from 15.76% on Hy4 preview to 5.45%, and improved 19.1% on its GameDev benchmark and 9.8% on GameLogic.

The licence is the part that changed. The Hy4 preview, released on August 28, shipped under Apache 2.0. Hy4 uses the Tencent Hy License, version 1.0, derived from Apache 2.0, whose main change is the commercial terms for large model-as-a-service businesses in Section 10. A business that offers the model as a hosted service needs a separate written licence from Tencent if its worldwide gross revenue tops $50 million in any 12 months, or before it uses the model in a product with more than 100 million monthly users. Internal use is exempt, and the licence counts fine-tuned, quantised, distilled, merged and pruned versions as derivative works. On safety, Tencent says it kept cybersecurity data in training to defensive material, and red teams from Tencent's Xuanwu Lab and Keen Security Lab found no elevated risk in reversing cryptography, defeating binary obfuscation or exploiting vulnerabilities. The model scored 30.4 out of 100 on Tencent's Hy-BioExperimentBench, which Tencent puts well below its high-risk threshold.

WHY IT MATTERSThe weights were the missing half of the launch

Hy4 launched on November 4 as an API-only model, and the weights were the part Tencent promised would follow. Open weights change who can use a model: researchers can inspect it, small teams can fine-tune it, and inference providers can serve it without going through Tencent's cloud. At 770 billion total parameters, Hy4 is among the largest open-weight models ever released, and its million-token context and vision and computer-use capabilities make it a general-purpose flagship rather than a narrow demo.

The licence is the counterweight. The August preview shipped under Apache 2.0, the most permissive terms in open source. The full model ships under a custom licence that asks the biggest commercial users to negotiate first. That is a deliberate break, and it reframes the release: the weights are public, but the right to sell them as a service at scale is not automatic. Whether that becomes a template for other frontier labs or a reason to pick a rival model is the question the release actually poses.

WHAT TO WATCHWatch the AMA and the first independent tests

Watch what the Hy team says in its live AMA on Reddit's r/LocalLLaMA, held 8 to 10 a.m. Pacific on November 20, including whether more of the Hy family follows and how the team defends the licence change. Watch second whether independent benchmarks confirm Tencent's own numbers, especially the coding gap against DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base and the hallucination-rate claim, which are the two figures most likely to move once the weights are in other hands.

Watch third whether vLLM and SGLang adoption and community quantisations follow as quickly as they did for Hy4 Flash, the cheaper 224-billion-parameter sibling whose weights were released days after its November 16 launch. At FP8 the weights alone run to roughly 770GB, so the practical question is not whether the model is downloadable but who can afford to run it, and whether the licence terms deter the large hosted providers that would make it widely usable.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISThe download is the news; the licence is the story

Tokenta Signal's judgment: Tencent kept its promise to open the weights, and the model is genuinely large and capable on its own numbers, with a million-token context and vision and computer-use capabilities carried over from launch. But the new licence draws a line the preview did not: the weights are public, using them at scale as a hosted service is a negotiation. For researchers and small builders that changes little; for the cloud providers who would actually serve a 770-billion-parameter model, it changes the deal. The honest limit is that every performance number so far is Tencent's own. Independent tests will decide whether Hy4 belongs beside the best open models or just beside its own launch chart, and the AMA and the first community benchmarks are the next evidence either way.
