Seedance 2.5 Flash API Goes Live, ByteDance's Faster Video Model at a Third Less

ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 Flash API opened November 15: the faster, cheaper video model is now live for developers on Volcano Engine's Ark platform, with BytePlus's international ModelArk opening the same day. At $0.152 per second of 720p video, about 34% below the flagship, it caps out at 720p and 30-second clips.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Volcano Engine and BytePlus announced Seedance 2.5 Flash on November 12, 2026, a lighter tier of the Seedance 2.5 video generator built by ByteDance's Doubao team. The company says generation runs nearly twice as fast at quality close to the flagship, and prices it about 34 percent lower: $0.152 per second at 720p, or $7.0 per million tokens at 480p and 720p, against $10.70 for the flagship.

The API opened on November 15. Volcano Engine's Ark documentation now lists the model as available for activation and API calls, under the model ID doubao-seedance-2-5-flash-261115, with no scheduled-opening language remaining on the page. BytePlus announced the same November 15 opening for its international ModelArk platform, where the model carries the identifier dreamina-seedance-2-5-flash-261115. The model has been selectable in Dreamina since November 12.

The limits are part of the product: Flash generates text-to-video and image-to-video at 480p and 720p only, with no 1080p or 4K modes, and a single clip runs up to 30 seconds. Each generation can take up to 10 images, 5 video clips and 5 audio clips as input.

WHY IT MATTERSA two-tier video market is now a priced product

ByteDance is formalizing the cheap, fast tier as a separate tariff with public pricing, not a settings toggle inside the flagship. Flash matches the price level of Seedance 2.0, the previous generation, which pushes the cost of iterating on video down to what the flagship market charged a generation ago.

That puts direct pressure on Kling and Runway to answer with their own cheap and fast tiers. The pattern is now a packaged workflow: check camera movement, composition and pacing in Flash, then regenerate the winning prompt in the flagship for a final 1080p render that Flash cannot produce.

WHAT TO WATCHThe speed claim has no public benchmark

Nearly twice as fast and quality close to the flagship are company claims with no published benchmarks, no baseline and no technical report on the acceleration method. Until independent measurements appear, the cost of the speed, in artifacts, motion quality or audio sync, is unassessed.

The other thing to watch is the rollout itself: the Ark documentation confirms the domestic side is live, and the international ModelArk opening was announced for the same day. Developer adoption in the first week will show whether the two platforms stay in step.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISCheap, fast video is now a line item, not a demo

Tokenta Signal's judgment: ByteDance is selling the iteration loop, not the final render. Flash is priced to be the place where ideas get checked and discarded, with the flagship kept for the one version that ships. The practical move is to build that two-tier pipeline from day one, and to treat the speed claim as marketing until an independent benchmark measures it.
