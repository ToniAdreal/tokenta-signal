Anthropic Greenshoe Exercised in Full, IPO Closes at $93.5 Billion

Anthropic said on Friday, November 20, 2026, that its underwriters had exercised the greenshoe option in full, lifting its IPO proceeds to roughly $93.5 billion from $81.34 billion. The option covered 147 million additional shares at $83, worth about $12.2 billion, and the offering has closed. With the $26 billion private placement, the full package is about $119.5 billion. WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

Anthropic said on Friday, November 20, 2026, that its underwriters had exercised the greenshoe option to buy additional shares, lifting the total proceeds from its initial public offering to roughly $93.5 billion, Reuters reported. A wallstcn feed carried by VolFlash reported at the same time that the IPO had closed and the underwriters had fully exercised the over-allotment option.

The option covered up to 147 million additional Class A shares at the $83 offer price, worth about $12.2 billion. Added to the 980 million shares priced on November 18 for $81.34 billion, it takes the public offering to $93.5 billion, the largest IPO on record. With the separate $26 billion private placement to seven technology companies, the full package comes to about $119.5 billion before fees.

The exercise closes the book on a three-day sequence that rewrote the record for public listings. Anthropic priced at $83 on November 18, above its $70 to $78 range, opened at $111.50 on November 19, and closed its first session at $97.17, up 17% from the offer price. The banks chose to take the extra shares from the company at $83 rather than cover their over-allotment in the open market, where the stock was trading above the offer price.

WHY IT MATTERSThe banks took the shares instead of buying them back

A greenshoe is a choice, not a formality. Underwriters oversell the deal and then decide whether to take extra shares from the company or buy them back in the market. Taking 147 million shares at $83, while ANT was trading above that level, says the banks were comfortable holding the stock at the offer price rather than scrambling to cover a short. It is the quietest vote of confidence in the whole transaction, and it came from the parties who know the order book best.

The exercise also locks in the headline number. At $81.34 billion the deal was already the largest IPO on record; at $93.5 billion it is larger by a margin no rival listing can close soon, and the $119.5 billion package with the private placement is the largest single capital raise by a private company going public. The record is no longer a one-day auction print. It is settled cash.

WHAT TO WATCHThe first quarterly report, and whether $83 holds

Watch first whether ANT holds above the $83 offer price in its second week. The stock closed its debut at $97.17, but it faded 13% from the open on day one, and the Asian sell-off that followed the debut showed the market is still arguing about whether AI spending earns a return. A break below $83 would turn the greenshoe from a demand signal into a top tick.

Watch second for Anthropic's first quarterly report as a public company, and for the first read on whether the $33.24 billion nine-month revenue line is catching the compute bill. The offering and the placement leave Anthropic with roughly $117 billion in net proceeds by the prospectus estimate. The question the market will ask from here is not how much Anthropic raised, but how fast it spends it.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISThe record is settled cash now, not an auction print

Tokenta Signal's judgment: the greenshoe is the part of the Anthropic listing that actually matters, and it arrived without drama. The $111.50 open was excitement; the $97.17 close was a verdict; the full exercise of the option is the banks putting their own balance sheet behind the $83 price. That does not settle whether Anthropic is worth its valuation, a roughly $2 trillion fully diluted tag against a nine-month operating loss. It settles something narrower and more useful: the demand at the offer price was real, the record is $93.5 billion, and the company now holds the largest war chest any AI lab has ever raised in one stroke. The spending starts now, and the spending is what the market will judge.

WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAINWhat Remains Uncertain

The exercise of the greenshoe and the closing of the offering come from Anthropic's own announcement on Friday, November 20, 2026, as reported by Reuters at 12:53 UTC and by a wallstcn feed carried by VolFlash at 12:47 UTC. The deal terms come from Anthropic's final prospectus: 980 million Class A shares at $83.00, an underwriters' option on up to 147 million more shares at the same price, and a separate $26 billion private placement. The $93.5 billion total is a gross figure before underwriting discounts and expenses; the prospectus puts net proceeds from the offering and the placement at approximately $117.0 billion if the option is exercised in full.
