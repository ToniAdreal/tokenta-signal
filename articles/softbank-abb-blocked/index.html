SoftBank's $5.375 Billion Deal Stopped in China

On November 5, ABB confirmed that China's foreign investment security review mechanism has blocked SoftBank's acquisition of ABB Robotics' China business. The $5.375 billion deal is neither cancelled nor cleared, and the two sides are discussing carving out the China business.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • 03THE CONFIRMATION — On November 5, ABB confirmed publicly that only the China business is affected.

WHAT HAPPENEDWhat Happened

ABB Robotics had 2024 revenue of $2.3 billion and EBITDA of $313 million. China is its largest single market, about 30%.

There is no official account of why the deal was blocked. The Financial Times, citing people familiar with the matter, said the concern was that robotics data could leave China and be used to train AI models, including OpenAI's. That is a report, not an official position.

SoftBank has been placing dense bets along the AI supply chain in recent years: Graphcore, Ampere, and DigitalBridge, then a stake of about 13% in OpenAI. ABB Robotics was the piece that would complete its physical AI picture.

CHINA ONLYOnly the China Business Was Blocked

ABB's statement is carefully worded. What China's foreign investment security review mechanism blocked is the acquisition of ABB Robotics' China business, not the whole deal.

Neither company has cancelled the deal; both say they remain in close communication and are pushing forward constructively. The deal hangs in mid-air: not dead, not alive.

THE CARVE-OUT PLANA Carve-Out Is the Way Out for the China Business

Bloomberg reported on November 7 that the two sides are studying splitting the China business into a standalone entity under a different owner so SoftBank can buy the rest. Talks are still early, and neither side has confirmed it.

Reuters reported on November 9 that SoftBank is talking with the relevant Chinese authorities. The split still needs approval after it is agreed, and nobody can guarantee it will work.

DEBT PILES UPSoftBank's Books Are Getting Heavier

SoftBank's net interest-bearing debt stood at ¥10.8 trillion, about $68.2 billion, at the end of June 2026, up from ¥6.6 trillion a year earlier. Note the timing: this is an end-of-June figure, not a new third-quarter result.

The shares fell first. They dropped 11% on November 6 and a further 4.2% on November 9, down 23% so far this year.

SIGNAL JUDGMENT — ANALYSISThe Block Hits One Business. The Logic Takes the Hit.

Tokenta Signal's judgment: with China at 30% of ABB Robotics, carving it out means repricing whether the deal is still worth $5.375 billion. SoftBank's AI supply-chain puzzle has been reshaped directly by a review mechanism for the first time.

The reasons for the block come from people familiar with the matter cited by the press, not from an official account. The carve-out plan is unconfirmed by either side and still needs regulatory approval. The ¥10.8 trillion debt figure is data as of the end of June 2026, not a third-quarter result.
