Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to go.tokenta.space, signal.tokenta.space and signals.tokenta.space.

What we collect

Reading Tokenta Signal as a guest requires no personal data. If you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address.
  • If you sign in with Google, GitHub or X: the basic profile those providers share with us (your name, email address and profile picture), plus a provider account identifier.
  • Your plan status (free registered reader, or Signal Pro if you subscribe).

We do not collect passwords. Sign-in is handled by the provider you choose, or by a one-time code we email you.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session active across our pages.
  • To lift the monthly free-reading meter for registered readers.
  • To send the sign-in code and, if the service launches, account-related email. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Cookies and local storage

  • Two session cookies (ts_at, ts_rt) on the .tokenta.space domain keep you signed in. They are HttpOnly and expire after 1 hour and 30 days respectively.
  • Your browser stores a monthly reading counter (ts_meter) locally so the free-reading meter works for guests. It never leaves your device except as part of normal page operation.

Service providers

Accounts and authentication are processed by Supabase. Sign-in code email is delivered by Resend. The sites are hosted by Vercel. Each provider processes data only to provide its service to us.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from the account page; the session cookies are cleared.
  • To delete your account and its stored data, email us at team@tokenta.space from the address on the account and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
